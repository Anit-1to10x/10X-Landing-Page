# Headlines - 10x Vibe Marketing

Generated: 2026-01-16
Brand Voice: Modern, Approachable, Innovative, Clear
Target Audience: Professionals wanting to learn AI automation for marketing

---

## Recommended Headline

**"Master AI Marketing Skills in Hours, Not Months"**

- Why: Directly addresses the "no time" objection while promising specific, practical marketing skills. Clear outcome, specific timeframe implication, speaks directly to busy professionals.

---

## Strong Alternatives

1. **"The AI Marketing Skills Course Built for Marketers, Not Developers"**
   - Addresses "too complicated" objection head-on, differentiates from generic AI courses

2. **"Stop Watching Generic AI Tutorials. Start Automating Your Marketing."**
   - Agitates the problem of wasted time, offers clear solution

3. **"Learn AI That Actually Applies to Marketing—Nothing Else"**
   - Emphasizes the unique focus on marketing-only applications

4. **"AI Marketing Automation for Busy Professionals Who Don't Code"**
   - Hits "no time" and "too complicated" objections simultaneously

---

## Subhead Options

For recommended headline:

1. **"Practical, hands-on training designed for marketers. No coding required. Industry certification included."**
   - Explains HOW (practical training) + addresses objection (no coding) + adds proof (certification)

2. **"Join thousands of marketing professionals automating campaigns, content, and analytics with AI."**
   - Adds social proof element and specificity on what they'll automate

3. **"Step-by-step modules you can complete in your lunch break. Start saving 10+ hours per week."**
   - Addresses "no time" objection with specific benefit (10+ hours saved)

## Headlines Considered & Rejected

| Headline | Rejection Reason |
|----------|------------------|
| "Supercharge Your Marketing with AI" | Too vague - could apply to any AI marketing tool |
| "The Future of Marketing is Here" | Cliché, says nothing about the product |
| "Revolutionary AI Training Platform" | Jargony, company-focused, not user outcome |
| "Learn AI Today" | Too generic, doesn't specify marketing focus |
| "AI-Powered Marketing Made Easy" | Vague, buzzword-heavy |
| "Transform Your Marketing Career" | Generic, could apply to any marketing course |
| "The World's Best AI Marketing Course" | Company-focused claim, no specificity |
